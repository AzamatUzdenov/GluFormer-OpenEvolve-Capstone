Title: Reproducibility audit of HbA1c prediction with GluFormer and OpenEvolve

Reproduce and audit an OpenEvolve-selected HbA1c predictor built on frozen GluFormer embeddings using public Shanghai data, with a locked environment and a Quarto report.

This bounded capstone replays two fixed historical programs. It preserves the historical development evaluation and reports known identifier-joining and target-scaling defects. Corrected evaluation with matched-input baselines is a separate next stage. The full LLM search, GluFormer pretraining and independent clinical validation are outside this stage.
